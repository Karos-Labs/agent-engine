# ig-kit-startups-venture (startup, founder and venture programmes)

id: ig-kit-startups-venture · version 1.1 · layer: L2 (category pack) · clients: thepitchbydeel

Standards and parameter values only; no line claims an engagement lift. Industry statistics appear as evidence refs (one vote per account, `scale.md`); a statistic from fewer than ~8 accounts is TRY-level evidence and never the basis of a DEFAULT on its own.

## Params

archetype institution; involvement high; audience mixed (founders and investors); locale en. carouselShapes payoff+proof (2) / wordless photo sequence (5-10) / one line per slide to a turn (6-10); coverWords 5-8 (<=10 hard); interiorWordLane story 0-12 (best median 4), one dense reference slide allowed behind a 7-word cover; photoShareMin 0.6; devicesPerSlide 0 default, <=1 kit device, none on covers; graphicsMix kit-only minimal; H ~4.5% of height on interiors and 96-104px on the cover, T ~2% raised to >=44, S >=26 in tracked caps (rail and pills; 30 otherwise); iconBand 0-2 labelled line icons inside the kit's pills on interiors; manifesto one size; captionChars 300-450; hashtags <=4; emoji 0; register conversational and personal; questionForm none; askSet {none, save/share in caption, client-mandated pointer}; logo at the L1 equal-area size or none, never the parent brand; formatPrior carousel-led, reels ranked by views; aiImagery never; stock never; typeface register: no condensed all-caps; timeliness: archival 'then' family TRY, programme news only as a launch; measurement hidden likes -> views and Insights; peer set 13 accounts (a16z, join_ef, founderspodcast, sahilbloom, deelpitch...), median ER 0.288%.

## Lines

1. [DEFAULT][graphics] No device by default; at most one kit device per slide (a labelled icon inside a pill counts as it, 0-2 per 8 slides) and none on covers; a client's captured device vocabulary outranks this default (D-1). (evidence: DS-09; startup graphic_devices none +0.36 8+/0- p=0.008, the strongest signal in the set; boxes 2+/5-, icons 0+/4-)
2. [DEFAULT][imagery] Real people, rooms and stages only: no AI, no stock. (evidence: stock 0+/5- p=0.06; a16z 0 of 17 outliers illustrated, its one illustrated carousel 0.73x)
3. [DEFAULT][hook] The cover carries 5-8 words with a name, a number or a claim, the payoff visible on slide 1; no question and no 'N tips'. (evidence: best cover median 5 vs 8 weakest; a16z 9 of top 10 covers <=10 words)
4. [DEFAULT][layout] Story slides carry 12 words or fewer and a dense reference slide 35 at most (the L1 hard cap); explainers with 25-54 words do not spread. (evidence: a16z story slides 0-12; explainers 0/5 at 2x; series 1.0x to 0.46x)
5. [DEFAULT][format] Three shapes: payoff plus proof (2 slides), a wordless photo sequence (5-10), one line per slide to a turn (6-10). (evidence: SLD-01 thepitchbydeel; a16z archival median 9.5x, manifestos 4.7x)
6. [DEFAULT][design] Restrained type register: no condensed all-caps display; full-bleed photo with an overlaid headline is the house cover. (evidence: condensed 0+/9- p=0.004; full-bleed +0.17 8+/2-; distinct sizes 4+ 0+/5-)
7. [DEFAULT][language] Register is conversational and personal, never hype, never flat informational, no exclamation marks. (evidence: informational 1+/6-; muted 4+/7-)
8. [DEFAULT][language] The carousel ends on the idea; no follow card, and the call to action lives in the caption or is absent. (evidence: LANG-09; a16z end cards 0/11 at 2x vs 12/28; link-in-bio 2+/7-; captions with a CTA 0.59x vs 1.02x)
9. [DEFAULT][language] Captions run 300-450 characters, the first line restates the cover declaratively, at most 4 hashtags, no emoji. (evidence: LANG-12; a16z 0/72 emoji, 2/72 hashtags)
10. [DEFAULT][strategy] Topics are founders' and companies' origins, pitching as a craft and primary artifacts (a founder's own tweet or first website); glossaries, trend charts and VC mechanics are reference content that does not spread. (evidence: a16z archival 5/5 at >=2x, tweet carousels 2/2, reference 0/12; VC terms 0.46x)
11. [DEFAULT][strategy] Programme news spreads only as a big launch with partner co-posts; one offer and one interview a week, never a repeated flyer. (evidence: a16z Academy launch 2.65x vs routine 0.24-1.16x; TOP-01 thepitchbydeel)
12. [DEFAULT][strategy] Credit and source the founder's, judge's or partner's own media first; the Collab is the kit's strongest TRY. (evidence: TOP-05 narrowed; Collab 1.38x vs 0.85x; @getdeel co-posts 9.9-17.8k plays vs 354 solo)
13. [DEFAULT][cadence] Franchises rotate and a guest never stacks. (evidence: a16z explainer 1.0x to 0.46x over 5 editions; 3 Brockman posts in 4 days 0.14-0.42x)
14. [DEFAULT][process] Likes are hidden on the client and many peers: rank reels by views and carousels by Insights, never placeholder likes. (evidence: J-1; @deelpitch likes hidden since 2026-08-18, reel median 354 plays)
15. [DEFAULT][imagery] Talking-head and banner reels are demoted; the same founders in archival footage do 1.06-5.43x. (evidence: a16z talking-head 0/6 at 2x, median 0.19x)
16. [DEFAULT][strategy] Entry points: applications open and close, a regional final, a judge's question, a winner's path, pitch craft, a founder's first company from public record, prize mechanics in exact wording, backstage, a city spotlight, the first 'no'. (evidence: The Pitch pillars; a16z families)
17. [DEFAULT][process] Compliance: prizes and programme mechanics only in the client's exact wording, no implied endorsement by uninvolved famous people, licensed or own images only, never a global champion implied. (evidence: LANG-08; a16z do-not-copy 9)
18. [TRY][format] An all-video carousel of one event night from several angles, 6-7 MP4 slides, the only headline on slide 1, labels after. (evidence: X-13; music all-video 1.52, 6-7 video slides 2.02)
19. [TRY][strategy] A founder's first-person moment around the act of pitching on a real backstage photo, as a Collab. (evidence: X6; fintech emotional 7+/2-; client 5+/3-)
20. [TRY][format] A wordless photo sequence of the programme's own events with names in the caption. (evidence: X7; luxury wordless 5+/1-; 4 target accounts only)
21. [TRY][strategy] A verified winner's path: name plus one verified number plus the proof artifact, as a Collab, with brand and legal sign-off. (evidence: X-14; Stan named-person + number)
22. [TRY][format] Payoff on slide 1, stage or check photo as proof on slide 2. (evidence: SLD-02; DT 2 slides 1.17 vs 0.91)
