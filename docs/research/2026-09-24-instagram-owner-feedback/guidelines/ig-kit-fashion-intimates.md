# ig-kit-fashion-intimates (intimate apparel and everyday underwear)

id: ig-kit-fashion-intimates · version 1.1 · layer: L2 (category pack) · clients: hankypanky, kindlyyours

Standards and parameter values only; no line claims an engagement lift. Industry statistics appear as evidence refs (one vote per account, `scale.md`); a statistic from fewer than ~8 accounts is TRY-level evidence and never the basis of a DEFAULT on its own.

## Params

archetype brand; involvement low; audience B2C; locale en. photoShareMin 0.8 (premium) / 0.6 (value); slideCountBand 3-7; coverWords 0-10 (median 0), the post's only headline; interiorWordLane 0-8 (wordless by default); textSizes 1-2 per designed card; headlineTier medium 64-72px with support >=36px (kit override of the 96px cover floor down to the L1 minimum of 64px: the photo is the hook); iconBand 0-1 labelled icon row (fit, fabric, care) on a graphic card, never on photos; devicesPerSlide 0 as text containers; graphicsMix product-built only (swatch quiz, shoppable grid, cut-out choice board, leader-line label); captionChars house short (27-155 char medians) unless contest rules; askPolicy every post, rotated, finite or fit questions, keyword-DM <=1 per week on a named product with a fresh reason; giveaways rare and big; hashtags 0-3; emoji sparing; register emotional-personal, creator first person; questionForm finite_choice allowed; logo none on photos, the wordmark in one position (top or bottom centre) at the L1 equal-area size on graphic cards only; formatPrior neutral (reels <=15 s); aiImagery never; stock never; vividness floor near the best-cover median; timeliness: dated moments (National Underwear Day 5 Aug, holidays), launches; peer set 22 accounts (aerie, meundies, thirdlove, savagexfenty, skims, knix, parade, harperwilde...), median ER 0.026%; hidden-like accounts (Felina, Natori, teamkindly) ranked on views and comments.

## Lines

1. [DEFAULT][imagery] Real bodies across the size range in saturated product colour on candid faces; 0 AI, 0 stock, no desaturation. (evidence: vivid pixels 64% of best vs 39% of weakest; Aerie #NotAI pledge 45,841 likes; 0 of 144 tagged posts use AI or stock)
2. [DEFAULT][layout] Words go on the cover only (0-10, the post's only headline); interiors are wordless, every slide a new picture, angle or body, and the set closes on the clearest product-visible frame. (evidence: storm-lead L2-03; cover words median 0 on best and weakest)
3. [DEFAULT][language] Every post carries one explicit, answerable ask after the point is made, rotated so no formula repeats; a keyword DM at most once a week on a named product with a fresh reason. (evidence: LANG-10; comment prompt +0.32 11+/1- p=0.006; +20 percentile points in all 9 testable accounts; oeak's repeated ask fell to 0.5-0.8x)
4. [DEFAULT][language] Register is emotional and personal in a witty first-person creator voice; complete brand taglines under-index. (evidence: emotional 9+/2-; taglines 20% of best vs 30% of weakest; story hooks +0.18 10+/1- p=0.012)
5. [DEFAULT][hook] No post without a hook; hooks are a dated moment, a person or participation, not product explanation or how-to. (evidence: hook none -0.33 0+/7- p=0.016; product explanation and seasonal taglines 0.67-0.74)
6. [DEFAULT][graphics] No text containers; a graphic is a choice or shoppable grid built from product cut-outs, or at most one labelled icon row (fit, fabric, care) per post on a graphic card, never a spec-sheet infographic. (evidence: swatch quiz 10.69x, grid 2.09x, cut-out boards 2.91x; question vs statement 118 vs 7 likes)
7. [DEFAULT][font sizes] One or two sizes per designed card with a medium headline of 64-72px and support at 36px or more; the photo is the hook, so the 96px cover floor is overridden down to the L1 minimum of 64px. (evidence: C-2; owner C 'bigger text'; medium headline 6+/0- p=0.031 is TRY-level at n=6)
8. [DEFAULT][logo] No mark on photos; a wordmark sits in one position (top or bottom centre) at the L1 equal-area size on graphic cards only. (evidence: DS-10 hankypanky/kindlyyours; intimates logo null)
9. [DEFAULT][language] Captions follow the house short length (27-155 character medians); the category's long-caption lift is giveaway-driven and stays a TRY. (evidence: short -0.19 1+/12- and long +0.23 8+/0- both confounded by contest rules, Aerie 9,855 comments)
10. [DEFAULT][format] Format is neutral within accounts; reels stay under 15 seconds and carousels run 3-7 slides, long only when every slide is a photo. (evidence: reels 0.49 / carousels 0.51 / images 0.49 percentile; SKIMS 5+ slides 2.94x)
11. [DEFAULT][strategy] Creator and model content runs as Collabs, the product colourway sets the palette, and a joke is paid off by the product within one swipe. (evidence: storm-lead intimates kit; TOP-05)
12. [DEFAULT][hook] Finite-choice quiz questions are allowed on covers and cards. (evidence: LANG-13; intimates question 6+/3-)
13. [DEFAULT][imagery] Text on a photo is white, one size, in the bottom third over a real shade or a naturally dark area. (evidence: H-1; medium contrast -0.14 L1)
14. [DEFAULT][strategy] Entry points: a dated moment, a pack or colour drop, a fit problem solved, the size-range proof, a creator try-on, a cultural trend-jack, a maker or staff story, a restock, gifting season, a 'which one' quiz. (evidence: Hanky Panky and Kindly L3 topic lines)
15. [DEFAULT][process] Claims about fibres, certifications and fit come only from the client's truth table; no shaming of competitors, prices or the reader. (evidence: LANG-08; Kindly claim gates; Hanky Panky 'your last pair' ban)
16. [TRY][language] A 600-900 character story caption behind one named style in the creator voice, judged on saves per reach. (evidence: X8; music long caption 6+/0-; needs an owner call against the house short caption)
17. [TRY][format] An archival 'then' carousel from the brand's own dated archive with words on slide 1 only, closing on today's saturated colour. (evidence: X-08; a16z archival 5/5 at >=2x)
18. [TRY][imagery] A named maker on a dated occasion as a wordless workroom sequence. (evidence: X-09; luxury staff sequences)
19. [TRY][format] A personalisation carousel, one moment paired with one style per slide, closing on a numbered grid. (evidence: X-10; Mindtrip 'which X fits your Y')
20. [TRY][hook] A side-by-side comparison turned into a finite choice. (evidence: X9; B2B comparison 4+/1-, weak at 6 accounts)
21. [TRY][format] A candid moment then the product-visible frame as a 2-slide shape. (evidence: SLD-02; best posts median 2 slides vs 1)
