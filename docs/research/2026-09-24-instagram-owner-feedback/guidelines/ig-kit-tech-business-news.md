# ig-kit-tech-business-news (tech and business news media; Hebrew today)

id: ig-kit-tech-business-news · version 1.1 · layer: L2 (category pack) · clients: geektime

Standards and parameter values only; no line claims an engagement lift. Industry statistics appear as evidence refs (one vote per account, `scale.md`); a statistic from fewer than ~8 accounts is TRY-level evidence and never the basis of a DEFAULT on its own.

## Params

archetype publisher; involvement none; audience mixed; locale he (Geektime). formatPrior image 0.5 / carousel-2 0.35 / reel 0.15; slideCountBand 1-2 (more only when every slide adds a fact); photoShareMin 0.5 with the cover always a photo; interiorWordLane news 25-35 at one size (L1 lane; was 30-50); coverWords 5-9 as a TRY cap (hard total cap 20 on the card cover); H one bold size of 68-76px on 2-3 lines (was a ~4.4% line height) + optional overline at 0.6x (>=41px); T single size >=44px; S credit >=30px (Hebrew has no caps); iconBand 0-2 labelled icons per post, mirrored in RTL; captionChars 300-650; hashtags <=1; emoji 1 on line 1; register plain authoritative, dry; questionForm none on covers; askSet {house article pointer (not counted), comment_finite TRY}; devicesPerSlide 1 comparison readable in a second; graphicsMix editorial 1-3; screenshotsPerDeck <=2 as a receipt on slide 2 (DEFAULT); twoSlideShare 0.3; freshness 7 days; measurement window 24-48 h; upstream registry and first-mover logging DEFAULT (target 2 firsts a week); aiImagery concept-labelled; stock avoid; thirdPartyShareMax media-high with credits; peer set 12 Hebrew tech accounts + wired, themarker_online, morningbrew, visualcap, evolving.ai; median ER 0.187%.

## Lines

1. [MUST][language] The lead fact is 7 days old or less or its date is on the slide; news verbs never dress an old milestone. (evidence: LANG-08; Geektime 15-month stat presented as 'reached')
2. [DEFAULT][strategy] Every story carries a reader stake: a local price or saving, an availability date, something free, or a named person's story. (evidence: Geektime L3; news with a reader stake 8+/2- p=0.11; winners median 12.3x and 4.1x)
3. [DEFAULT][strategy] House programmes and partner content never run as news, and partners stay off the cover. (evidence: publisher promos Calcalist TECH1 0.09x, WIRED event 0.07x, Visual Capitalist webinar 0.04x; partner logos 31.9% weakest vs 12.5% best)
4. [DEFAULT][hook] The cover headline is named actor + active verb + consequence on 2-3 lines; the 5-9-word cap is a TRY, not a rule, because the set's best covers run longer. (evidence: H-2/LANG-17 geektime; cover words median +3, 9+/2-)
5. [DEFAULT][hook] An open question is not a hook; ask only after the fact is delivered. (evidence: LANG-13; open questions 5.6% of best vs 13.9% of weakest)
6. [DEFAULT][format] Single image by default; a carousel is a 2-slide hook-then-receipt or one new fact or voice per slide, never a slide that restates the cover. (evidence: SLD-02/SCR-01 narrowed; hook-then-receipt 26.5x and 7.61x; feed 75% single images)
7. [DEFAULT][imagery] The cover is photo-dominant in this order: own product photography, vendor press, a screenshot receipt, a labelled concept last; never a generated scene of a real event or person. (evidence: photo-dominant +0.27 10+/1- p=0.012; product photo 5+/1-; IMG-04 geektime)
8. [DEFAULT][design] Headlines sit inside the house card or plate, never raw on the photo, and there are no full dark or muted plates. (evidence: medium contrast 0+/8- p=0.008; dark 1+/9- p=0.02; muted 0+/8-; high contrast 6+/0-)
9. [DEFAULT][graphics] One comparison readable in a second per story: a giant numeral, a before-and-after price, a ranked bar, or the source document as slide 2, with up to 2 labelled icons mirrored for RTL; illustration is the last rung. (evidence: GFX-01/GFX-03/GFX-05; big number 3+/0- and illustration 1+/6- are TRY-level at n<8)
10. [DEFAULT][process] Upstream registry as the sixth topic engine: company newsrooms, investor-relations pages, exchange and SEC filings, the national innovation authority, importer price pages, founders' and VCs' accounts; a story is saturated when 3 competing desks ran it in 7 days unless the angle is new. (evidence: TOP-04; DT upstream sources 3.5-4.9 days ahead of aggregators)
11. [DEFAULT][language] Captions run 300-650 characters in ~12-word sentences with digits, a one-line hook first, and close on the article pointer plus the photo credit; the pointer is a house line, not the ask. (evidence: LANG-12/LANG-09 geektime; tech news medium +0.18 6+/1-; link-in-bio 3+/3- null)
12. [DEFAULT][process] Measure after 24-48 hours at least, use comments where likes are hidden, and never rank a post under 96 hours on a busy page. (evidence: J-1; Calcalist 'worst' were 4-12 h old)
13. [DEFAULT][format] Reels run only with native footage, never sponsored clips. (evidence: reel -0.27 2+/8-; Doom native footage 13.36x)
14. [DEFAULT][strategy] Credit named people and companies and source their own media first; the Collab invite is a TRY. (evidence: TOP-05 narrowed; named-person stories median 4.11x)
15. [DEFAULT][strategy] Entry points: a launch or price in the local market, funding or layoffs with a named person, a regulation change, a device in hand, an exit anniversary, a reader-facing feature, local availability, a security incident, a hiring wave, a gadget comparison. (evidence: Geektime winners; X-01/X-02)
16. [DEFAULT][process] Compliance: press images credited in the post language, the AI label in the credit line, a 'scoop' label only for true scoops with a stake, no publisher reach metrics on slides. (evidence: LANG-08 geektime; ORIG-01)
17. [DEFAULT][process] First-mover from the upstream registry: post first, log every news post as first or follower with origin age, target 2 firsts a week, and review after 3 weeks against a 1.5x median over followers. (evidence: X-02 promoted from logging-only; DT 3.25x first vs 0.49x four hours later)
18. [TRY][strategy] Story-behind anniversaries from the national tech canon (exits, IPOs, launches, founding dates verified against filings) with a present-day stake, one a week for 5 weeks (the L1 5-posts-per-arm read). (evidence: X-01; DT story-behind 40% of outliers, median 3.5x; nostalgia saturates 29% to 24%)
19. [TRY][language] A named person's emotional stake quoted in their own words on the cover within the freshness gate. (evidence: X5; fintech emotional 7+/2-; tech news 5 accounts only)
20. [TRY][language] One concrete, easy comment ask after the fact. (evidence: LANG-10 geektime; generic 'what do you think' does not separate winners)
21. [TRY][format] Stickers or emoji marks on covers, recorded only. (evidence: stickers/emoji +0.29 3+/1-, n=4)
22. [TRY][strategy] One defended position a week on a named source (a regulation, a platform policy, a study), stated as the desk's reading, as the comment lane. (evidence: Q-4 transfer from Don Techno; DT's only two 6,000+ comment posts were a study and a policy)
