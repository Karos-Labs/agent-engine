# ig-kit-music-nightlife (music media, scene pages, nightlife; from the Don Techno system and 9 tagged pages)

id: ig-kit-music-nightlife · version 1.1 · layer: L2 (category pack) · clients: dontechno

Standards and parameter values only; no line claims an engagement lift. Industry statistics appear as evidence refs (one vote per account, `scale.md`); a statistic from fewer than ~8 accounts is TRY-level evidence and never the basis of a DEFAULT on its own.

## Params

archetype publisher (person if run as one DJ's voice); involvement none; audience B2C; locale en. photoShareMin 0.9-1.0 (footage); textSizes 2 sans + 1 small tracked mono source/date line; cover lockup: eyebrow 'source · date' <=35 chars at the S size / kicker <=22 chars at the T size (~48px) / NAME largest (120 -> 96px then wrap, so NAME:kicker <=2.5 and NAME:eyebrow <=4); body slide: eyebrow + one 60px headline on 70px lines, <=4 lines, >=36px; proof slides: footage with a top label only; colour from the footage, no accent, no devices, no charts; graphicsMix and iconBand 0 (footage-led: nothing covers the footage); scrim always on (clear above ~42%, ~88% black at the edge) plus a top veil for the mark; logo one fixed corner, no handle, index or credit on the plate; canvas 1080x1440 cards, 1080x1920 reels; shapes: 2-slide claim+proof (twoSlideShare 0.25), all-video carousels 6-10 clips (videoSlideShare 0.3 TRY), reels 10-20 s or 60-90 s (avoid 20-30 s, unstable, re-measure per client); footage gates in code (720p floor, 1080 export, YAVG >=20 in the first 2 s, brightness >=55, no stickers, watermarks, ads or AI); captionChars long, story in order, credit line mandatory, ~2 hashtags; askSet none (credit line instead); register plain authoritative; questionForm statement; timeliness weekly targets carousel current 3 / evergreen 1 / nostalgic 1 / bridge 1 and reel 3/4/3/2 scaled to cadence, nostalgic brake at 35%, why-now within 7 days; dedupe same clip never, storyline 60 d, entity 2 d; doors: virality >=3x the author's own median age-projected, sweet spot >=1.10 of own median; position lane <=1 a week on a named source; measurement comments where likes are hidden (22-77% of posts); peer set 9 pages (don.techno, defected, mixmagmagazine, 6am_group, technoandhousemusic, boilerroomtv, ra_news, cercle, edmmusic), median ER 0.114%.

## Lines

1. [MUST][imagery] Third-party clips appear only with a credit line and a collaborator invite (the invite doubles as the permission request); publishers use own, partner or licensed footage; music on business accounts comes from the licensed library or the original upload; no AI media presented as real footage or news. (evidence: dt.json hard_rules; ORIG-01/FMT-02)
2. [MUST][process] A hidden like count is never read as a number; comments carry the measure on pages where likes are hidden. (evidence: J-1; 22-77% of posts hide likes on the 9 pages; DT 82 of 102 posts)
3. [DEFAULT][design] The pack's convention is photo-led with colour from ungraded footage: no accent colour, devices, charts or icons over the footage (graphicsMix and iconBand 0). (evidence: devices 6 uses across 54 best posts on 9 pages; bright designed colour 0+/6- p=0.03 and dark 5+/1- are TRY-level at n=6)
4. [DEFAULT][font sizes] Two sans sizes per slide plus one small tracked mono source-and-date line (a declared label mono may set the date there, C-5), NAME at 120px at most, white caps, left-aligned lower third, never over a face. (evidence: Q-3/DS-02 dontechno; distinct sizes 0-1 4+/0-; the origin of the owner's two-sizes rule)
5. [DEFAULT][design] The bottom gradient is always on under type (clear above ~42% of height, ~88% black at the edge) with a light top veil for the mark and labels. (evidence: H-1 dontechno; a16z 7-34/255 behind text)
6. [DEFAULT][language] One plain spoken sentence of about 10 words naming the subject and the moment; authoritative scene knowledge wins and hype loses; questions in 2% of top posts. (evidence: authoritative +0.26 7+/0- p=0.016; hype -0.36 0+/7- p=0.016; the same clip 0.35 vague vs 2.95 named)
7. [DEFAULT][format] The 2-slide claim-plus-proof card beats 3-5 slide carousels within every page that posts both; all-video carousels of one night lead the format table. (evidence: 2 slides 1.17 vs 3 slides 0.91 (4/4 pages); all-video 1.52, 6-7 video slides 2.02, n=45)
8. [DEFAULT][format] Reels run 10-20 s or 60-90 s, never 20-30 s, and the band is re-measured per client because it flipped between periods; the whole moment is kept and only dead air trimmed. (evidence: 10-20 s 1.21 n=41; 60-90 s 1.60 n=35; 20-30 s 0.74 n=71; UNSTABLE)
9. [DEFAULT][strategy] The story behind a well-known record, looked up in its documented history and posted on its anniversary with one little-known fact, is the strongest lane. (evidence: 40% of outliers, median 3.5x; track or sample origin 2.13)
10. [DEFAULT][strategy] Watch one step upstream (talk shows' own channels, newsrooms, promoters, venue shooters), post first, and penalise a story once 3 tracked pages ran it in 7 days unless the angle is new; re-check a trend before committing. (evidence: TOP-04; first 3.25x vs 0.49x four hours later; IG-born stories reach big pages 3.5-4.9 days late)
11. [DEFAULT][strategy] Every reachable subject gets a collab invite and a credit line; the subject's own media comes first. (evidence: collab 1.37 vs 0.98; artist-owned collab reels 4.26)
12. [DEFAULT][hook] Each post carries a timeliness class with a why-now for throwbacks (anniversary within 7 days, trending again, played this season, the artist active now); nostalgia saturates. (evidence: bridge 1.37, nostalgic 1.17, current 0.99; aggregators' nostalgic share 29% to 24%)
13. [DEFAULT][cadence] Dedupe windows are measured on the page's own repeats: the same clip never, the same storyline 60 days, the same artist 2 days. (evidence: CAD-02; storyline cost 16% inside 60 d, artist 33% at 1-2 d, nothing from day 3, on 1,074 posts)
14. [DEFAULT][language] Captions are long, tell the story in order, carry the mandatory credit line and about 2 hashtags, and force no ask. (evidence: long +0.29 6+/0- p=0.03; medium 1+/6-; no CTA 14% vs 12%)
15. [DEFAULT][strategy] Ticket and lineup promos, logistics reposts, link digests, gig recaps of unknown names, posed portraits and talking-head reels sit at the bottom of every tagged page; plain single images work only for news. (evidence: promos 1.09; single image 0.21 n=26; 'AI ranks the 5 best' listicles 0-4 comments)
16. [DEFAULT][strategy] Comments come from opinions and science, not clips: one defended position post a week on a named source is the comment lane. (evidence: the only two 6,000+ comment posts were a study and a platform policy; 'has X sold out?' 564)
17. [DEFAULT][imagery] Footage gates run in code before any vision call: 720p floor, 1080 export, bright face-on footage, crop plus scale <=1.5x, faces kept in the 3:4 crop, no stickers, watermarks, ads or AI labels. (evidence: QUAL-01; dt.json quality_gates)
18. [DEFAULT][process] The market's taste is not the page's taste: a peer's winning format enters only through a door the page's own history confirms. (evidence: EDM mainstage 1.05 market vs 0.33 on DT; nostalgia 1.17 vs 0.70)
19. [DEFAULT][strategy] Entry points: a record's anniversary, a sample's origin, a celebrity crossover from the source channel, a place as the headline, an unexpected person hearing a track, a night from several angles, a scene rule change, a platform policy, a chart re-entry, a festival closing track. (evidence: dt.json formats table)
20. [DEFAULT][process] Compliance: DMCA pulls honoured, 'via @' credit for UGC, no ranking gimmicks, human posts only (draft-only, a person publishes from the calendar). (evidence: dt.json hard_rules)
21. [TRY][strategy] A scene rule-change explainer as a 2-slide card with the catch on slide 2. (evidence: X-15; Nath law explainers 31.5x)
22. [TRY][language] A scene-culture question as the last caption sentence on anniversary posts, and a finite tally question with a follow-up count; both need the owner's sign-off because they conflict with the no-ask rule. (evidence: X12/X-16; intimates comment prompt 11+/1-)
23. [TRY][format] Video slides inside carousels at a 0.3 share, measured through the own-grid loop. (evidence: FMT-03; image-then-video 1.32)
