# facet-archetype (closed list of 7; standards card, not a category pack)

id: facet-archetype · version 1.1 · layer: L2 (facet card) · clients: karoslabs: business, geektime: publisher, thepitchbydeel: institution, hankypanky: brand, kindlyyours: brand, sitti: platform, xodigital: platform, dontechno: publisher, n3: place

Standards and parameter values only; no line claims an engagement lift. Industry statistics appear as evidence refs (one vote per account, `scale.md`); a statistic from fewer than ~8 accounts is TRY-level evidence and never the basis of a DEFAULT on its own.

## Params

owns: voice_person, face_on_camera, produced_vs_ugc_look, audio_source (hard: read from the IG account type via API), cadence_default, collab_style, news_peg_handling, series_affinity. Tie-break: the voice the account actually posts in (first-person human = person; editorial or curatorial = publisher). Adding a value needs a registry PR approved by Albert.

## Lines

1. [DEFAULT][language] brand (consumer product brand): voice = the brand in first person plural or a creator first person; face on camera = customers, models and creators across the size range; produced look with candid moments; collab_style = creators and models as Collabs; news pegs only via cultural moments; series affinity medium (drops, quizzes). (evidence: robustness-v2 archetype skill +0.0085 p=0.01; taxonomy judge:taxonomy)
2. [DEFAULT][language] place (local business, venue, property): voice = the house, sober third person; face on camera = staff and consenting people living the place, never guests; produced look; collab_style = non-competing partners with consent; news pegs = season and official dates; series affinity high (fixed weekday lanes). (evidence: N3 client rules 12-19; luxury set: staff sequences, faces +0.27 6+/3-)
3. [DEFAULT][language] person (creator, artist, personality, educator): voice = first-person human; face on camera = the person, candid and in action; UGC-grade look accepted; collab_style = venues and brands as Collabs; news pegs = the person's own moments; series affinity medium; 'educator/advisor' folds in until 5+ benchmark accounts exist. (evidence: taxonomy closed list; Sitti founder reels 14x followers)
4. [DEFAULT][language] publisher (media, news, curation): voice = editorial 'we' or faceless; face on camera = the subject of the story, never the desk; produced look with the subject's own media credited; collab_style = named subjects and sources; news pegs = the product itself with a freshness gate; series affinity low to medium (numbered weeklies as TRY); audio_source = licensed library or original upload on business accounts. (evidence: Geektime and DT syntheses; publisher promos 0.04-0.09x)
5. [DEFAULT][language] platform (consumer app, software, marketplace): voice = the product's people in second person to the user; face on camera = the named creator or customer, the founder for reach; produced look with real product UI as the only device; collab_style = the creator or customer whose story it is; news pegs = rule changes and city events; series affinity high (fixed skeleton per series). (evidence: Sitti and XO L3 rules; creator-app product-as-graphic)
6. [DEFAULT][language] business (B2B company, agency, service): voice = conversational first person plural of the team, one decisive strategist; face on camera = named people from case subjects, archival; produced look, graphic-led interiors; collab_style = the featured brand or person only when a partner agreed; news pegs = live events by name; series affinity high (named numbered series). (evidence: Karos July grid; B2B conversational 7+/1-)
7. [DEFAULT][language] institution (programme, event, competition, community): voice = the programme addressing founders or members, warm and specific; face on camera = participants, judges and winners on real stages; produced look from the programme's own archive; collab_style = judges, verified winners and partners as Collabs; news pegs = application dates, finals, launches; series affinity high (pillars). (evidence: The Pitch L3 rules; @getdeel co-posts 9.9-17.8k vs 354 plays)
8. [MUST][process] audio_source is a fact read from the Instagram account type (business vs creator) and is never overridden by a lower layer; business accounts use the licensed library or the original upload only. (evidence: FMT-02; Meta music licensing for business accounts)
9. [DEFAULT][cadence] cadence_default by archetype: publisher follows the news flow (Geektime daily, DT 18/week bespoke); brand, platform and business 3-5 a week; institution 5-6 around its season; place 2-3 a week bound by asset supply. (evidence: CAD-01 per-client extents)
