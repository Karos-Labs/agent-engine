# ig-kit-marketing-advertising (B2B marketing / AI agency)

id: ig-kit-marketing-advertising · version 1.1 · layer: L2 (category pack) · clients: karoslabs

Standards and parameter values only; no line claims an engagement lift. Industry statistics appear as evidence refs (one vote per account, `scale.md`); a statistic from fewer than ~8 accounts is TRY-level evidence and never the basis of a DEFAULT on its own.

## Params

archetype business; involvement high; audience B2B (mix 50:50, named-company proof); locale en. photoShareMin 0.3-0.4 (photo covers, graphic interiors); graphicsMix data-led 2-4 per 8 slides from a shared device library; interiorWordLane editorial 15-25 (max 35); slideCountBand 5-7; coverWords 3-9, <=7 on photo covers; H cover 96-110 with T 44 and S 30 on the cover (H:T <=2.5, H:S <=4), interior 64-72 same position every slide, T >=44, S 30 (26-28 only for tracked caps), one stat size only as a lone figure; iconBand 1-3 labelled icons per 8 slides in the client's icon style, 0 on covers; tables in the captured table style; captionChars 90-180 words in 3-5 paragraphs; askSet {save-for-X, keyword-DM, none} optional and rotated; hashtags <=5 from a topic pool; emoji 0; register conversational; questionForm answered; headlineTier large (huge-short TRY); devicesPerSlide 1 interior / 0 cover; logoPresence every slide (cover-off TRY); accentAreaMax 5%; formatPrior carousel 0.7 / reel 0.3 (TRY); aiImagery concept-labelled; stock never for people, avoid otherwise; screenshotsPerDeck <=2 never slide 1; timeliness: live news pegs by name, campaign anniversaries TRY; peer set 14 B2B accounts (nogood.io, orenmeetsworld, sintra.ai, peoplebrandsandthings, thebrandblueprint_, reputeforge...), median ER 0.279%.

## Lines

1. [DEFAULT][strategy] The subject is a named brand, campaign or person with a live peg; house work enters at most 1 post in 3 and as the output, never the pitch (internal posts are the weakest pillar in B2B accounts). (evidence: TOP-01/LANG-04 karoslabs; J-5)
2. [DEFAULT][imagery] Imagery comes from archival and public-domain sources (Wikimedia Commons, Library of Congress, Smithsonian), press and plain product shots, credited on the slide and in the caption; stock is avoided and never used for people. (evidence: IMG-04/IMG-05; storm-lead B2B kit stock lift 0.29)
3. [DEFAULT][graphics] Each interior slide is a picture of the point: one graphic that carries the fact (real asset, real UI, found ad, diagram, side-by-side comparison) beside 15-25 words. (evidence: storm-lead L2-02 comparison lift 3.33; owner M)
4. [DEFAULT][graphics] Covers carry no icons, charts, screenshots or big-stat devices. (evidence: B2B icons 1+/6-, boxes 2+/7- scale.md; storm-lead cover graphics 0.4-0.5)
5. [DEFAULT][graphics] Interiors add labelled icons by default at 1-3 per 8 slides in the client's icon style (an icon row for a 3-step chain, a chip beside a stat, a feature list), each counting as the slide's one device. (evidence: GFX-01/GFX-03; owner M 'add icons'; B2B icons 1+/6- is TRY-level at n=7 and measured unlabelled decoration)
6. [DEFAULT][graphics] Devices are hairline cards with one accent mark each and no gradients; the accent covers at most about 5% of the slide's pixels. (evidence: D-1 karoslabs; storm-lead accent-area param; owner 'orange stays on data fills')
7. [DEFAULT][language] Register is conversational B2B with no hype and no informational flatness. (evidence: B2B conversational +0.25 7+/1- p=0.07, informational 2+/6- scale.md)
8. [DEFAULT][language] Captions stay short to medium (90-180 words, sourced inline, series label allowed) and are not lengthened for this audience. (evidence: LANG-12; B2B long 4+/6-, short +0.17 3+/1-)
9. [DEFAULT][hook] A cover states a claim; a question appears only when the same slide answers it. (evidence: LANG-13; B2B question 2+/4-)
10. [DEFAULT][strategy] Comparisons are shown side by side, AI products are shown through their output, and live news is pegged by name. (evidence: storm-lead B2B kit; L2-02)
11. [DEFAULT][strategy] Entry points: campaign anatomy, brand origin story, a pricing or positioning move, an AI tool's actual output, agency craft, a named marketer's career, then-versus-now pairs, an owned data study, a live event or launch, a hiring decision. (evidence: Karos series set; a16z archival 5/5 at 2x)
12. [DEFAULT][process] Compliance: archival media is credited on the slide and in the caption, no trademark is used as an icon, and no client's work is shown without permission. (evidence: ORIG-01/GFX-03)
13. [DEFAULT][format] Format prior is carousel-led with reels only as TRY slots until the peer test shows the audience is reel-trained. (evidence: FMT-01 karoslabs; peers 1.41-1.80x on carousel-led vs 0.37-0.56x on reel-trained audiences)
14. [TRY][logo] Cover without the lockup versus the lockup on every slide, read on the benchmark since the client's own account cannot score it. (evidence: DS-11; B2B none 8+/1- p=0.04, top-left 1+/7-)
15. [TRY][font sizes] Huge headline on covers of 7 words or fewer versus large. (evidence: DS-04; B2B huge 7+/1- p=0.07)
16. [TRY][design] A bold brand accent in one device versus a quiet version. (evidence: DS-07; B2B brand-colour heavy 5+/1-; storm-lead L1-19 killed at L1)
17. [TRY][language] Emotional or personal register and story hooks, untested in this set. (evidence: P-1; fintech source 7+/2-; B2B emotional 0.0 over 5 accounts)
18. [TRY][graphics] An owned-data series: one checkable finding per carousel from the benchmark, the number inside a sentence on a real image pair, code-built charts, n stated, no causal words. (evidence: X-03 narrowed once; Wired and Visual Capitalist data journalism)
19. [TRY][format] A typographic manifesto of fact-then-consequence pairs, one sentence per slide at one size on alternating grounds. (evidence: X-04; a16z manifestos 3/4 at 2x)
20. [DEFAULT][process] Campaign launches come from an upstream registry (brands' own newsrooms and channels, agency case pages, award shortlists, trade launch feeds), and every news-pegged post is logged as first or follower with the origin's age. (evidence: Q-2/TOP-04 transfer from Don Techno; DT upstream sources 3.5-4.9 days ahead of aggregators, first 3.25x vs 0.49x)
21. [TRY][strategy] One defended position a week on a named source (a campaign result, a pricing move, a platform policy), the argument in the caption, as the comment lane. (evidence: Q-4 transfer from Don Techno; DT's only two 6,000+ comment posts were a study and a policy)
