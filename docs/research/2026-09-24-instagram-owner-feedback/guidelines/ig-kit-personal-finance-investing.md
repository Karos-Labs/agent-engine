## Lines

1. [MUST][language] Every regulatory claim carries its status and date; a proposal is badged 'not in force' on every slide that mentions it with a 'but note' slide stating today's rule; returns are 'up to X% a year' and past returns are labelled past; the disclaimer and registration line close every caption; regulator marks are never decoration. (evidence: LANG-08/GFX-04 xodigital; the run presented a CVM proposal as in force)
2. [DEFAULT][hook] The cover states the reader's stake as one checkable sentence (a rule with its date, a rate with its minimum, a named person with a number), never a market-size or listicle count. (evidence: storm-lead L2-04; cover words best 9.5 vs 13; market-size covers 0.56)
3. [DEFAULT][language] Complete conversational sentences in the local language with the catch before the reassurance; no hype, and emotion is not forced. (evidence: conversational 30.6% vs 19.4%, 6+/2-; hype 0.55; Brazil emotional 1+/4-)
4. [DEFAULT][design] One heavy condensed or geometric display face with a plain body face as the pack's house pattern (a serif-and-sans mix is not the default); two sizes plus one meta size per slide. (evidence: C-1; condensed display +0.29 7+/1- p=0.07; serif+sans mix 0+/3- is TRY-level; 4+ sizes in 30.6% of the weakest)
5. [DEFAULT][graphics] Data graphics carry the point: stat pills with an icon, value and label, ranked logos of named entities, before-and-after bars, a 01/02/03 row; decorative pills and text-only plates lose. (evidence: data graphics lift 3; stat pills with icons 2.67; other brands' logos 6+/1-; pills 1+/5-; text-only plate 1+/5-)
6. [DEFAULT][graphics] Boxes and cards exist only as house containers carrying a value, and a client whose captured design language is device-rich (XO's rows, badges and stat pills) keeps its own devices over this pack's no-device default; the international set punishes decorative boxes hardest. (evidence: fintech & investing boxes 0+/8- p=0.008; none 11+/2- p=0.02; DS-09 narrowed: DEFAULT here)
7. [DEFAULT][imagery] The subject itself is photographed (the card machine, the price tag, the printed document, a named consenting investor) or the house plate carries a real chart; stock finance scenery and people never appear, and generated imagery only as a client-owned character. (evidence: stock 0+/6- p=0.03 international, 1+/4- Brazil; photo-dominant flat 5+/4- in Brazil; chart cover 5+/1-)
8. [DEFAULT][format] Interiors carry a body of 25 words or fewer plus one graphic and one new fact each on one fixed layout with one pacing device, 5-15 slides, closing on the biggest number, the document, or the CTA with the disclaimer. (evidence: storm-lead fintech kit; news lane 33 words best)
9. [DEFAULT][language] Captions are long and extend the cover's one idea, with the disclaimer and registration line always present. (evidence: long >600 chars 50% vs 36%, 8+/3-; LANG-12)
10. [DEFAULT][language] The ask is save or share on reference content; link-in-bio is negative; a comment question is a TRY only about knowledge or experience, never the reader's finances. (evidence: save/share 3+/1-; link-in-bio negative; LANG-10 fintech TRY; X10)
11. [DEFAULT][strategy] Borrowed authority appears in text (the regulator, the exchange, the press), never as their logo. (evidence: authority in text 15.3% vs 2.8%; regulator logo cropped as decoration in the run)
12. [DEFAULT][strategy] Spine formats: the rule-change explainer (today / proposal / but note / what to do), the monthly proof report, one offer per post with the benefit as the headline, hard-sell only on a hook. (evidence: Nath law explainers 31.5x; storm-lead fintech kit)
13. [DEFAULT][process] Upstream registry: the official gazette, regulator resolutions and working groups, central-bank resolutions, the tax authority, small-business rule pages; a freshness gate dates every rule; compliance review precedes any position post. (evidence: TOP-04/Q-2 xodigital; X-06)
14. [DEFAULT][process] Measurement uses saves and sends per reach and non-follower reach, because small regulated accounts cannot rank designs on likes. (evidence: J-1; @xodigital.oficial 385 followers, carousels flat at 5-18 likes)
15. [DEFAULT][strategy] Entry points: a rule or limit change, a rate with its minimum, how a named small business raised money, what a receivable is, the platform's guarantees, a monthly proof number, a tax deadline, a scam warning, the app flow, a plain-language glossary term tied to a rule. (evidence: XO L3 spine; INCO and Nath patterns)
16. [DEFAULT][process] Compliance: no imagery implying guaranteed returns, no personalised recommendations in replies, no giveaways, consent for any named person, registration numbers never drawn as statistics. (evidence: LANG-08 xodigital; X4/X10 risks)
17. [TRY][imagery] An object-photo cover under the house scrim versus the brand plate, as topic-matched pairs. (evidence: X2/X-05; luxury photo-dominant 10+/0-, tech news 10+/1-; Brazil fintech 5+/4-)
18. [TRY][strategy] A rule change posted within 24 hours as a 2-slide claim-plus-proof with the official text as the receipt. (evidence: X-06/SLD-02; DT first-mover 3.25x vs 0.49x)
19. [TRY][strategy] A named, consenting investor's or issuer's decision story with one checkable number that is never a return. (evidence: X4; creator story hooks 6+/1-)
20. [TRY][strategy] A client-owned character as the recurring host of a proof series, never next to a rate. (evidence: X-07; XO capybara reels 9,902 views on 385 followers; Sintra owned character)
21. [TRY][font sizes] Huge heavy display on covers, 2 lines at most. (evidence: DS-04; Brazil huge 7+/2-)
