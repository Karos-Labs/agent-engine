## Lines

1. [MUST][design] Relais Display for headlines and Fabriga for everything else, weights 400/500 with Relais italic for emphasis; no Georgia, Helvetica Neue, monospace or bold; until the client supplies files and a webfont licence the client is on FONT_HOLD and nothing publishes, unless Albert signs off a named same-genre substitute for this client, shown on the gate card of every post. (evidence: A-1/A-2 hankypanky; the run set Georgia, Helvetica Neue and IBM Plex Mono because the portal read Yotpo's widget CSS)
2. [DEFAULT][font sizes] One two-step scale per post: headline 64-72px (raised from the client's ~56px B6 for legibility), support 36-40px, at most two sizes on a slide, no display numerals or stat figures; on-image copy is 8 words or fewer per slide and 25 per carousel, everything else in the caption. (evidence: DS-02/DS-03/M-2 hankypanky; the run used nine sizes from 220 to 16px)
3. [DEFAULT][imagery] Client-library photography only, in saturated Signature Lace colours with candid laughing faces in real rooms or outdoors; nothing desaturated, no AI people or lingerie, no stock, no collages. (evidence: storm-lead L3-03; best covers median 64% vivid pixels; the client's own collage 0.46x; slide 2 of the run was AI lace)
4. [DEFAULT][design] No boxes, cards, panels or gradients: a text-only slide sits on one flat colourway ground, and text on a photo is white Relais in one size in the bottom third over a real shade to ~45% black or a naturally dark area, never dark text on a washed-out photo. (evidence: DS-08/H-1 hankypanky; the run put dark text on a white-washed photo)
5. [DEFAULT][graphics] Graphics are product-built and sparse, one idea per slide: a multi-colour flat lay or grid of real thongs with an offer, a swatch quiz with the game visible using the site's colour names and hex, or a lace close-up with at most three thin leader-line labels at the support size, or one labelled icon row (fit, fabric, care) on a flat colourway card at most once per post; no spec-sheet infographics. 7. [DEFAULT][format] Carousel-first, 3-6 slides: open on a candid moment, change setting or angle on every slide, close on the most direct camera-facing product frame; pack and price go in the caption, never on a recap slide. (evidence: FMT-01/I-2 hankypanky; own carousels 1.15x vs reels 0.71x, n=16/17; slide 8 of the run restated slide 1)
