# ig-kit-travel-local-discovery (creator economy, city guides, local discovery apps)

id: ig-kit-travel-local-discovery · version 1.1 · layer: L2 (category pack) · clients: sitti

Standards and parameter values only; no line claims an engagement lift. Industry statistics appear as evidence refs (one vote per account, `scale.md`); a statistic from fewer than ~8 accounts is TRY-level evidence and never the basis of a DEFAULT on its own.

## Params

archetype platform; involvement low; audience mixed (creators and map users); locale en. photoShareMin 0.7; slideCountBand 6-8 (cover, 4-6 spots, closer); coverWords <=8 (best 6 vs 10); interiorWordLane <=8 on-photo words plus the venue name label; textSizes <=2 (title + tag at 1.5-2:1) plus the lockup; cover title >=96px (text cover, no photo-hook override); floor 36px normalised for the face's x-height; headlineTier huge-short TRY; devicesPerSlide product only (app card, pins, map, route, save count); iconBand the product pin on every interior plus 0-1 labelled product icon row (route, save, price) on the app card; decorative pills off; upstream registry for openings with first-mover logging; captionChars 1-2 lines plus venue @-tags; hashtags 1-3; askSet {one fixed closer tied to the list, one content question}; link-in-bio never the hook; register story and emotional-personal; questionForm none for open questions; logo by slide type as kit files, never a handle; formatPrior reels for reach under 1K followers, carousels for series; aiImagery never; stock only for weather or season; hot-news mode off; timeliness: home-city and seasonal pegs, venue anniversaries TRY; measurement: drop hidden-like and <72 h posts, reels on views, discount comment-keyword funnels; peer set 13 accounts (mindtrip.ai, wherethefuckdowego, mapstr, infatuation, stanforcreators...), median ER 0.19%, ~30% of the weakest set are placeholders.

## Lines

1. [DEFAULT][strategy] A named place or person plus one concrete promise wins; pitching creators to monetise is the category's weakest format. (evidence: storm-lead L2-06; 'monetise your recommendations' 0.15-0.23x; Sitti run scored a platform framework)
2. [DEFAULT][language] Register is story or emotional-personal, never hype or a neutral listing, as the category's best accounts write. (evidence: LANG-05 at L1; story lift 4.19; emotional 5+/0- p=0.06, hype 1+/5-, neutral 0+/5- are TRY-level at n=5-6)
3. [DEFAULT][hook] The cover carries one idea in 8 words or fewer, a named place, person or city plus one promise, on a card, a darkened band or cream with the photo framed, never raw on a busy photo. (evidence: cover words best 6 vs weakest 10; DS-12 sitti)
4. [DEFAULT][layout] Each interior slide is one named place on a real photo of that place with 8 or fewer on-photo words and a neighbourhood tag, in a fixed skeleton per series with a different place and photo on every slide (I-2). (evidence: L2-06; place/scene photos 41 of 71 best)
5. [DEFAULT][graphics] The product itself is the graphic: the app card, save count, map, route or chart, with the product's own pin, route and save icons labelling every place; decorative pills and unlabelled icons stay off. (evidence: GFX-01/GFX-03 sitti; pills 1+/6- and icons 0+/3- are TRY-level at n=3-7)
6. [DEFAULT][imagery] Real candid photos of the actual place or person; stock only for weather or season; no AI people. (evidence: IMG-02/IMG-05 sitti; stock-looking men in the run)
7. [DEFAULT][font sizes] One or two families, at most 2 sizes with one dominant short line, a text cover title at 96px or more and nothing under 36px normalised for x-height; a huge short cover stays a TRY. (evidence: C-2/DS-03 sitti; huge 4+/1- is TRY-level at n=5)
8. [DEFAULT][design] Grounds follow the client's captured palette (Sitti's cream and coral system); a dark-plate direction is not a pack default and runs only as a TRY. (evidence: DS-07; dark 0+/5- p=0.06 is TRY-level at n=5; muted +0.11 reverses)
9. [DEFAULT][language] One fixed closer tied to the list plus one specific content question; link-in-bio is never the hook, and a save/share ask is not the default. (evidence: comment prompt 7+/1- p=0.07; LANG-09 link-in-bio at L1; save/share 0+/3- is TRY-level)
10. [DEFAULT][language] Captions are short with venue @-tags on their own lines and 1-3 hashtags. (evidence: LANG-12 sitti; storm-lead creator kit)
11. [DEFAULT][format] Founder-POV and discovery reels carry reach under 1K followers; carousels carry the repeatable series. (evidence: Sitti founder reel 8,620 views = 14x followers; FMT-01 sitti)
12. [DEFAULT][strategy] Earnings stories only with a real named creator and real numbers; the founder's build-in-public story until one exists. (evidence: Stan 746 comments on a named creator; GFX-04 sitti)
13. [DEFAULT][process] Measurement drops hidden-like and under-72-hour posts, compares reels on views and discounts comment-keyword funnels. (evidence: J-1; 30% placeholders in the weakest set)
14. [DEFAULT][strategy] Entry points: a creator's map of a city, no-line spots, a neighbourhood's best, seasonal openings, a dish worth the trip, a hidden bar, a local's Sunday, a city event, a route, 'which one did she miss'. (evidence: Sitti L3 topic rule; mapstr and infatuation patterns)
15. [DEFAULT][process] Compliance: creator-supplied photos with permission, venues @-tagged and credited, no invented app values. (evidence: ORIG-01/GFX-04 sitti)
16. [DEFAULT][process] Openings, closings and seasonal menus come from an upstream registry (venue and restaurant groups' own accounts and newsrooms, city licence and openings lists, local openings columns), and a named place posted first is logged as first or follower. (evidence: Q-2/TOP-04 transfer from Don Techno; DT upstream sources 3.5-4.9 days ahead)
17. [TRY][strategy] A named local creator's authoritative verdict per city versus a neutral listicle. (evidence: X11; music authoritative 7+/0-; target 6 accounts)
18. [TRY][strategy] The named owner or bartender behind the place as a three-way Collab with the app place card inset. (evidence: X-11; luxury staff sequences; Collab lifts in music and startup sets)
19. [TRY][graphics] A ranking built from owned data as the whole graphic with a credibility count. (evidence: X-12; Visual Capitalist ranked charts)
20. [TRY][hook] A text-free cover with the line one swipe in, and a numbered episodic series. (evidence: P-1 sitti; 6amgroup 4 of top 6)
21. [TRY][language] One content question as the comment ask, never a tag request. (evidence: LANG-10/LANG-11 sitti)
