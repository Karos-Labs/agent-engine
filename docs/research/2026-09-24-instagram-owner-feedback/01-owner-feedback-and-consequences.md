# Owner feedback, 2026-09-24, and its consequences

Albert reviewed eight Instagram carousels from Tomer's prep runs of 24 September (7 clients). Each feedback point below lists the root cause the code audit found, the consequence (what changes), and the workstream in [02-build-plan.md](02-build-plan.md) that carries it. **Status: every workstream is planned; no engine code is in this commit.** Wave 1 (fonts, duplicate slides, the site-identity guard and the parameter table) was started and then paused to package this round. Its partial diffs are kept locally and will be finished on this branch.

## Decisions the owner needs to make

1. **Logo corner default:** top-left (top-right for Hebrew), or top-right placed below Instagram's counter?
2. **Uploaded logo vs lab file** when the upload is a screenshot (Karos, Sitti, XO): does the latest deliberate human choice win, or a matching lab file plus staff approval?
3. **Label faces:** Karos (Hanken uppercase, JetBrains Mono or DM Mono) and XO (the locked kit or the live site)?
4. **Commercial fonts** (Bagoss for Deel, Relais/Fabriga for Hanky Panky, GT Walsheim for Kindly): who supplies the files and a licence that covers images? Until then these clients render flagged free substitutes.
5. **Covers:** make the capped full-bleed poster with a measured shade the default and #244's framed picture the fallback?
6. **Imagery:** graphics before generated pictures, and AI imagery as a per-client policy (replacing the three-generated-frames guarantee)?
7. **Kindly Yours:** confirm the domain and category, and approve deleting its 14 contaminated context docs.
8. **Storage:** delete the 29 orphaned `slide-8.png` objects and add a lifecycle rule (needs an account with access to the prep media bucket).
